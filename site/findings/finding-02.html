Critical limitation

Why this finding carries no percentage

The other two core findings are counts of field states in a defined record universe. This one is not. It is the result of a search of the public record, and a search result is bounded by what was searched. There is no denominator here because the question is not "how many of N," it is "was a complete series located." It was not.

That distinction is the whole finding. An absence in a searched record is evidence about the searchable public record. It is not evidence that the underlying obligation went unmet, and this site does not present it as such.

The obligation

Ordinance 125054, Section 9, required annual reporting beginning June 15, 2017 on activity connected to the tenant-protection provision. The ordinance text and the related Rental Registration and Inspection Ordinance annual reports that were located are held in Reports & Source Records.

What was located

Two Rental Registration and Inspection Ordinance annual reports were located and are preserved with the source records: the 2022 report and the 2024 report. These are RRIO program reports. Locating them does not satisfy, and is not offered as satisfying, the Section 9 obligation — they are a different reporting instrument, and this site does not treat one as evidence about the other.

What would change this finding. A complete located series, or a City record establishing where the Section 9 reports were published, would change it. STLCA has filed public-records requests on adjacent questions; that correspondence is preserved in the source records. If reports are produced, this finding is revised — that is the standard, not an exception to it.
