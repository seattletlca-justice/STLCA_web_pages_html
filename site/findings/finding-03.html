Records with no recorded inspection result, by district

The district extracts report the count of records with no recorded LastInspResult against each district's own record count. Note that the district denominator here is the full district record count, not that district's closed/completed subset — so these shares are not directly comparable to this finding's headline share, which uses the closed/completed denominator.

How closure statuses are distributed

Across the district-assigned universe, the great majority of records carry a terminal status. The finding is not that records stay open — it is that a terminal status frequently arrives without a recorded inspection result attached to it.
