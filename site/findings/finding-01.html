The same gap, cut by district

Every district shows the same pattern. The share of records with no recorded LastInspDate ranges across the seven districts, but no district is an exception to the documentation gap. Bars are direct-labelled and the underlying counts are available as a table.

What the field actually contained

The reason this is a documentation finding rather than an inspection finding is visible in the source exports themselves. In public exports reviewed before July 2025, the LastInspDate column carried geographic point geometry rather than dates. The Addison on 4th case study shows this at building level, across that property's full record history.
