Case study

What the inspection-date field contained

Finding 01 rests on a documented defect in the LastInspDate field: before 2025 it was not functioning as a historical inspection-date field, and carried point geometry or was left blank. That is normally an argument made at the scale of tens of thousands of records. This building shows it in a form anyone can check by opening one CSV.

Every value in that column, for this address, is a coordinate pair — the same coordinate pair, repeated, because it is the location of the building rather than the date of anything. The field is not sparsely populated here. It is fully populated with the wrong kind of data.

Scope of this case study

This page is built only from the SDCI code-complaint and violation record for this address. Other material about this property exists in the project archive, including narrative documents that assert causation and institutional fault. Those claims are not established by the record analysed here, are not repeated on this page, and are held for separate human review before any publication. The discipline is the same one applied everywhere on this site: the record supports what it supports.

One building is an illustration, not a population Nothing about this property's record generalises to other properties. Its value is that it makes a field-level defect concrete and independently checkable, not that it is representative.
