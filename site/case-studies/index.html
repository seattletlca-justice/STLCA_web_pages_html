Case Studies

One building at a time

A citywide finding is hard to check. A single building's record is not. These case studies take one property, reconstruct its enforcement history from the public record, and state plainly what that history establishes — and where it stops.

How a case study is bounded

A case study illustrates; it does not generalise. One property's record cannot establish a pattern across the city, and this site does not use it that way. Its job is to make a field-level or process-level observation concrete enough that a reader can verify it against the same public export.

Names come from the record A person is named only where the name appears in a public record, and each name stays attached to the record it came from. Material that asserts individual fault beyond what the record shows is held for separate review rather than published.
