Dashboards and dossiers are the same house style, on purpose. Dark navy is for screen reading and scanning. The findings, case studies and methodology pages are white and print-first, because they are built to be printed and attached to a records request or a letter to an oversight body. The two styles are held apart deliberately.
Unresolved: dossier versus dashboard. The build direction records an open question — each district was to have both a dossier and a dashboard, reading the same data, with the split between them never defined. Rather than build fourteen pages against an undefined split, this site ships one district page per district. That decision is flagged for review, not settled.
