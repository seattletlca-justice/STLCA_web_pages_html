NOV-to-complaint record ratio

For each year, the count of violation records divided by the count of complaint records opened in the landlord-tenant universe. The ratio is much lower after 2017 than before it. What that change means is not established by this chart, and this page does not assert a cause.

This is a record ratio, not a rate The figure is violation records divided by complaint records within a year. It does not track whether any particular complaint led to a violation — the source record does not link a complaint to a resulting violation, so no conversion rate can be computed from it. Reading this as "the share of complaints that produced a violation" is the single most likely way to misuse the chart below.

The ratio over time

The two counts behind it

The ratio moves for two reasons at once, and separating them matters. Complaint records rise sharply from 2018. Violation records do not fall to zero — they stay broadly within their historical band while the complaint count multiplies. Both series are record counts in the same unit, so they share one axis.

Before and after

What this chart does not establish It does not establish that enforcement performance changed, that inspections stopped, or that any obligation went unmet. Record-keeping practice, record-type usage, intake routing and complaint volume all move these counts. An abrupt change in a published field grouped by year is a documentation observation. Treating it as a performance measure would require independent supporting evidence that this site does not have.
