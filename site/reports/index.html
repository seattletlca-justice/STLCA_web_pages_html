Follow the record

No public number without provenance. A figure published here is tied to a source snapshot, a record universe, a date scope, an extraction date, a QA status, its exclusions, and its limitations. This page is where the first of those lives. Files are held in the repository alongside the site, not on a server — the paths below are relative to SOURCE_RECORDS/.

Governance documents

The rules this site is built under are themselves part of the record. They are held in GOVERNANCE/.

  • README_STLCA_SITE_GOVERNANCE.md — editorial frame, the fixed findings, evidence and publication rules, and the R.A.R.E. review model.
  • README_DATA_AND_DENOMINATORS.md — source of truth, the coexisting universes, and denominator discipline.
  • WEBSITE_BUILD_INSTRUCTIONS_20260807_v01.md — build gates, prohibited language, content controls, and QA requirements.
  • README_BUILD_HANDOFF.md — the handoff notes accompanying the build instructions.

Archive

Superseded pages, earlier template generations, duplicate trees and packaging snapshots are retained in _ARCHIVE/ rather than deleted, with ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.csv recording where each item came from and why it was moved. Material whose claims exceed what the record supports is held in _ARCHIVE/review_required/ pending human review.
