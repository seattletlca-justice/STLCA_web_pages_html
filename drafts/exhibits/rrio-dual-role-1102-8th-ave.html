What the record shows

  • City portal status pages assign the investigation, warning, NOV preparation, NOV issuance, NOV final review and violation withdrawal steps at this one property to the same named individual, from 2015 through 2024.
  • A City-issued certificate shows the same name registered as a private RRIO inspector, valid through August 22, 2025.
  • That certificate was printed five weeks after the same individual performed NOV final review for the City.

What the record does not show

  • The date the private registration was first issued.
  • Whether any City post-employment or conflict-of-interest rule applied.
  • Any finding of wrongdoing by any body. No motive is asserted here.
  • Whether this pattern extends beyond this property. Assignee names appear only on portal status pages, not in the published datasets.
